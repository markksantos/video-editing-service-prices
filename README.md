# Video Editing Service Prices

Published prices of 35 video editing services (editing subscriptions, per-video services, full-time editor plans, podcast editors, design subscriptions that include video, thumbnails), each read on the provider's own pricing page on October 25, 2026, plus 15 figures calculated from them (median entry price, price per included video, billing discounts).

Published by [Mark Studios](https://www.markstudios.com) with the full write-up, method and limitations at **https://www.markstudios.com/research/video-editing-service-prices**.

## Files

One row per figure: stat_id, claim, value, unit, population, geography, period, publisher, source title and URL, locator, publication and access dates, whether the figure is reported or calculated, status, method notes and licence notes. PRC rows are published prices; DRV rows are calculations whose inputs are listed in source_locator.

Files in `data/` are copied from the live page on 2026-10-25; the page is the source of truth and is re-verified quarterly.
